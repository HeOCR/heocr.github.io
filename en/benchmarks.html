P-mod-open v0 results

31 pages · 65 images · 1,863 reference lines · Download the benchmark dataset · Cite the dataset (Zenodo DOI)

Official gazettes, legal notices and other public documents in modern unpointed Hebrew. A scanner and two phones captured the same 17 printed pages, scored against the same text for each page. Other images come from archives or publishers’ PDFs.

The primary metric is page character error rate (CER).

P-hist-open v0.1

18 pages · 30 image regions · 973 reference lines

Public-domain scans of pre-modern Hebrew print without vowel marks. The benchmark scores selected regions of the scanned pages.

The primary metric is page character error rate (CER).

HeOCR P-hist v1.1 led the registered comparators in both views, with 14.33% CER across all eligible text and 14.07% after filtering.
