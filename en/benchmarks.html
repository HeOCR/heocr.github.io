HeOCR benchmarks

How HeOCR reads real Hebrew pages, next to other open tools. Every figure gives its sample size, a 95% interval, the measure, the reference and the run it comes from. The page is built from one file, results.json, which is updated after each scored run.

Every figure on this page comes from photographs or scans of real pages. Generated (synthetic) documents are used only to develop and select models, and their figures are not reported.

Modern printed Hebrew

Scored

Printed pages photographed by phone (PS-v1)

12 pages of printed modern Hebrew (legislation and prose), typeset by us from public-domain texts, printed and photographed by the maintainer with three phones. Held out: no model was trained, tuned or chosen on them. Character error (lower is better) per condition; the first photo of each page per phone counts.

On these real photos, heocr's own model is no better than stock Tesseract's model inside heocr, and heocr is slightly worse than plain Tesseract on flat photos. It finds more of the numbers.

Pointed print (niqqud) photographed by phone (NQ1)

25 pages of modern pointed Hebrew (poetry, Tanakh, and prose pointed by Dicta's nakdan), typeset by us in open fonts, printed and photographed twice in each of two sessions. Held out. Character error is shown with niqqud counted, and with niqqud removed from both sides (letters only); marks found is the share of vowel marks read correctly.

HeOCR's own models write no niqqud: pointed text comes out unpointed, for now. On the letters alone, the default model matches stock Tesseract's model.

Method, reference and run
  • Measure: page CER with niqqud kept and with niqqud removed on both sides, under line_fold_v1; marks found = reference vowel marks matched on the aligned letter
  • Interval: 95% cluster bootstrap over works (2,000 resamples, seed 0)
  • Run: niqqud_print_v1_2026-11-19, amendments 1–3; one registered run 2026-11-20

Handwriting

Scored 2026-11-20

225 handwritten Hebrew lines by 10 writers, photographed by phone, from ivrit.ai's Hebrew handwriting OCR benchmark (test split). Held out: no HeOCR model was trained, tuned or chosen on them. Each reader gets each line image as it is.

Handwriting is not supported yet. On these held-out lines every reader, ours included, gets most characters wrong. HeOCR's own model, trained on print, does worse here than stock Tesseract's model (+6.5 points of character error, 95% interval +4.5 to +8.7).

No HeOCR model is trained on handwriting. The reference text is ivrit.ai's volunteer transcription, not checked by us. With 10 writers, intervals are wide. Word error can pass 100% when a reader writes more wrong words than the line has.

Data: ivrit.ai Hebrew handwriting OCR benchmark (Hugging Face), from transcriptor.ivrit.ai; CC BY 4.0 under the ivrit.ai licence; held privately for evaluation only; not covered by any HeOCR licence.

Method, reference and run
  • Measure: line CER and WER under line_fold_v1, ratio of sums over all 225 lines, blank outputs included; words found = mean share of a line's reference words present in the output
  • Interval: 95% percentile bootstrap over the 10 writers (2,000 resamples, seed 0)
  • Run: ivritai_hw_lines_v0_2026-11-20; one registered run 2026-11-20 (heocr 0.2.0rc1; heocr 0.2.0 (identical to 0.2.0rc1, verified by wheel diff): byte-identical outputs, addendum 1)
