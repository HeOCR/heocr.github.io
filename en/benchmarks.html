P-hist-open v0.1

Public-domain scans of historical unpointed Hebrew print. The set contains 18 leaves, 30 image regions, and 973 reference lines.

The primary metric is page character error rate (CER). Lower is better. The deciding views use 875 eligible lines and 837 lines after excluding known training-text overlap.

HeOCR P-hist v1.1 had the lowest CER among the registered comparators in both deciding views.
