Datasets

Each dataset is built only from material that can be redistributed and reused, including for machine learning. Rights are recorded for each item, not assumed for a whole collection.

HASH

Hebrew Archive of Scanned Handwriting: scanned handwritten Hebrew-script documents from the 18th century onward, including letters, notebooks, drafts and forms.

htext

Public-domain Hebrew text for training, starting with lines from Project Ben-Yehuda works, plain and with niqqud. Each work is flagged as public domain in Israel and/or the US.

Sources & credit

We use public-domain works only, and each item keeps its source and credit. See rights & takedown for how rights are checked.

National Library of Israel

Scans of public-domain books from its "public domain, free use" collection. Credited on every item as "From the collections of the National Library of Israel / מאוסף הספרייה הלאומית", with the record number.

Internet Archive & Wikimedia Commons

Public-domain book scans made by university libraries, such as the University of Toronto, and by Google for libraries. Each item keeps its identifier, who scanned it and its rights evidence. Google's cover pages and watermarks are removed.

Project Ben-Yehuda

Volunteer-typed, public-domain texts of the same editions. They supply the exact text for each scanned line, and the training text for synthetic pages.

Hebrew Wikisource

Proofread transcriptions of scanned pages. We use them for evaluation, with each page checked by a human. Their text is CC BY-SA.

Synthetic Hebrew page generator. It renders printed and handwriting-style pages, with line annotations and right-to-left metadata, from CC0 training text and open fonts.

Code signing policy

For the HeOCR desktop app.

Windows: free code signing provided by SignPath.io, certificate by SignPath Foundation. Only installers built by GitHub Actions from the public source are signed, and each signing is approved by hand. macOS: signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Team roles: committers and reviewers, Shay Palachy Affek (@shaypal5), with AI coding agents that work through reviewed pull requests; approver, Shay Palachy Affek.

Privacy: this program will not transfer any information to other networked systems unless specifically requested by the user or the person installing or operating it. The app has no network feature at all. The full policy.
