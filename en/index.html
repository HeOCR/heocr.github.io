HeOCR

HeOCR is an open project for getting Hebrew text out of any document: PDF and Office files, web pages, scans and photos. The code, the models and the data are open, and the tools run on your own computer or phone.

Sources & credit

The releases use public-domain or openly licensed sources. Each item keeps its source and credit. rights & takedown for how rights are checked.

National Library of Israel

Scans of public-domain books from its "public domain, free use" collection. Credited on every item as "From the collections of the National Library of Israel / מאוסף הספרייה הלאומית", with the record number.

Internet Archive & Wikimedia Commons

Public-domain book scans made by university libraries, such as the University of Toronto, and by Google for libraries. Each item keeps its identifier, who scanned it and its rights evidence. Google's cover pages and watermarks are removed.

Project Ben-Yehuda

Volunteer-typed, public-domain texts of the same editions. They supply the exact text for each scanned line, and the training text for synthetic pages.

Hebrew Wikisource

Hebrew Wikisource text appears in the training corpora and in line pairs. Releases link to source revisions and record the CC BY-SA terms.

Code

Tools

heocr

Python package and CLI: Hebrew text out of PDFs, Office files, web pages, scans and photos.

heocr-desktop

HeOCR's apps for Mac, Windows and Android: Hebrew text out of documents, fully offline.

Models and data

heocr-engine

The models: training and packaging Hebrew OCR models.

htext

Public-domain and openly licensed Hebrew text, for training Hebrew OCR.

hocrsyngen

A generator of synthetic Hebrew OCR training data: lines and pages.

hdocsyngen

A generator of synthetic Hebrew documents: Office files and PDFs, with exact structure.

Review and infrastructure

heocr-review

Check and correct Hebrew text against page scans, line by line.

heocr-compute

Runs HeOCR's training jobs on rented machines: open data only, within a budget.
